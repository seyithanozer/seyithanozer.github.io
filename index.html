Why doesn't England's space standard get used?

Introduced in 2015, adopted by fewer than half of local authorities. This research traces the institutional conditions that resulted in this outcome. →

How much of England's new affordable housing meets the national standard?

An analysis of 9,876 newly built homes across 153 developments show that only 43% comply nationally. →

Does London's existing housing meet current space standards?

A machine learning analysis of 2,283 floor plans from inner London shows that 88% fail at least one room-level dimensional requirement. →

What does it mean for a home to be sufficient?

The issue is rarely the size. What makes a home sufficient depends on who lives there. for sharers, the bedroom was the primary private space; for families, the kitchen-living area was the heart of the home. For both, what was missing was overspill space. →

What do home alterations tell us about housing design?

Many terraced houses in London have been extended. What people change reveals what original designs achieved and failed to provide. →

Does a good home have to look like this?

New housing in England defaults to the same carbon-blind typologies decade after decade. This project asks whether public engagement can unsettle the expectations that hold that pattern in place. →
